# Support

| Tier | Channel | P1 response | Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community | GitHub Discussions | best effort | — |
| Pro ($19) | support@wah-lah.com | 48 h | 12 months of updates |
| Team ($99) | support@wah-lah.com | 24 h | 12 months of updates, named contact |
| Commercial Buy-Out | priority@wah-lah.com | 72 h security SLA | 12–36 months of updates |
| Enterprise | named engineer | 4 h | 12-month LTS branch |

## LTS
`release/1.x` receives security fixes for 12 months past the last 1.x minor. Upgrades
between minors are always expand/contract safe.

## Not supported
Forked servers, custom drivers we did not ship, and anything behind a reverse proxy you
configured. We will still help — just not on an SLA.

## Escalation
P1 = production down or data loss. P2 = degraded, workaround exists. P3 = cosmetic.
State the severity in the subject line; we honour the label you apply.
